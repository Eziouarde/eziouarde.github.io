Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Summary

Auto Poster2Louis is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else.

2. Data we access

When the owner authorizes the app with "Login with TikTok", the app receives:

• Basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) – scope user.info.basic

• Permission to publish videos to the owner's account – scope video.publish

• An access token and a refresh token issued by TikTok

3. How the data is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account and to check the publishing status. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer only. They are never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API servers (open.tiktokapis.com). No database or third-party service is used.

5. Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions), and all locally stored tokens are deleted when the app is no longer used.

7. Children

The app is not intended for and does not collect data from children.

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version is always available at this address.
